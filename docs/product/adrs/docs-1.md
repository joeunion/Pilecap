# ADR docs-1

**ADR docs-1 · a shipped document's claims are derived or resolved.**

**Context.** Five senior reviewers read the 2.5.0 export and found the mechanisms sound and the prose behind them: `architecture.md` calls a landed rung the future (`:94`), counts 26 rules where the gate prints 98 (`:4751`), maps an app never built (`:3592`) and names itself the canonical ADR home beside a second one (`:3601-3608`); runbooks disagree with their owning files; 796 `path:line` cells in the shipped docs are checked only for being inside the file; the threat census "can only tell you the file is long enough". The one derived set, `docs/sdk/`, regenerated byte-identical and every spot-checked citation resolved — the pattern already exists in the tree and the prose outside it has no instrument.

**Decision.** A claim in a shipped document is one of three things, and the gate reads all three: **(1) derived** — a fence `<!-- derived: <kind> -->…<!-- /derived: <kind> -->` regenerated by `python -m quality.sdk --blocks` from static sources and `--check`ed in `--fast` (the app map, the rule count, the ADR index, the alert census, the deploy step count; a product registers kinds in `quality/local/blocks/`); **(2) a citation that resolves** — `path::name` parsed, `path:line` refused past EOF or on a blank line, both by `scripts/dangling_paths.py` over every SHIP file, and a mechanism cell in the threat model is `::name` or refused; **(3) a decision cited by id** — an `ADR <app>-<n>` that resolves to `docs/product/adrs/<app>-<n>.md` (one file per ADR, the only home) or a house rule `--rules` prints. Anything else about the tree's future — "not yet", "planned", "when … lands", a ledger id or a `reports/` path the export lacks — is refused in a current-state file by `current_state_census.py` (a census `scripts/` gains), with no valve: the sentence moves to the ADR's context or to the queue. `architecture.md` becomes current state (stack table one ruling per cell with its ADR id, the doors, the gate, the house rules, one derived app map); the decision log is the directory. `publish.sh --release` refuses a minor or major whose candidate tree no critique report names in its header; a patch is advised.

**Consequences.** `--fast` runs three more static checks (sub-second); 796 cells are migrated once by a rewriter and corrected by hand where they drifted; 38+ ADR blocks move by a committed script; the ADR census re-points from two files to a directory (two one-line edits: `scripts/docs_legs.sh:77`, `scripts/archaeology_lib.sh:129`); a release of a minor costs one five-lens read (17 minutes wall, dollars unmeasured). A product that clones inherits the fences, the census and the directory, and its own pages opt in by carrying a fence.

**Rejected.** A heading marker for the current-state boundary (a hand-placed marker over an open set). A valve or excusal grammar for the tense census (rule 91's shape already is deletion-or-an-id). One `decisions.md` (unreadable at the same length). Deriving the app map from `introspect` (Django and a database in the hook). The critique refusal on every patch (the wall). Leaving `adrs-ingredients.md` as a second home (the census must have one directory to resolve into).

**Trigger to revisit.** A derived kind needs Django or a database to compute — then the `--check` moves to the loop and the fence says `derived at the loop:` in its opener; or a product needs a fourth claim kind the three do not cover.
