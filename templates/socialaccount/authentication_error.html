{% extends "account/base_entrance.html" %} {% comment %} The page a federated sign-in lands on when the round trip itself failed — a state mismatch, an expired authorization code, a provider that answered an error, a callback that did not verify. allauth's own copy says "An error occurred while attempting to login via your third-party account." and stops. That is a sentence with no fact and no move in it: the person cannot tell whether they mistyped something, whether they are not allowed in, or whether the deployment is broken, so the only thing left to do is press the same button again. This page says which of those it is not, and gives them the one move that is theirs. It is deliberately the SIBLING of `accounts/sso_refused.html` and not a duplicate of it, because the two are different facts: that page is *we declined to make your successful sign-in a session here*, this one is *the sign-in did not complete at all*. Both are reachable by anybody, so neither names a tenant, a connection, or whether an account exists. The `` back to the sign-in page is safe here where `sso_refused.html` deliberately has none: nothing was decided about this person, so trying again is a reasonable move rather than an invitation to repeat a refusal. {% endcomment %} {% load i18n allauth %} {% block head_title %}Sign-in did not complete{% endblock head_title %} {% block content %}

Sign-in did not complete

The sign-in was not finished, so you have not been signed in and nothing about your account was changed. This is usually a link that was opened twice, or one that sat unused for too long.

Start again

If starting again lands here a second time, ask whoever administers your account: something on the identity platform's side needs attention, and nothing you can do here will fix it.
