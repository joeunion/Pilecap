{% comment %} FRAME `reset` — design J5's forgotten-password road, as M6 settled it. THE DESIGN DREW A PAGE WHERE THE PERSON PICKS A NEW PASSWORD. They cannot, and the measurement is why: the identity platform's reset mail carries a link to the PLATFORM's own page (`/ui/login/password/init`), with an empty `authRequestID` — a mail has no application context, so the per-application login-v2 declaration that moves the sign-in screen cannot move this one (`zitadel_measure`'s M6, against v4.17.1, read out of a real inbox). A frame of ours with a New-password field on it would have been a screen nobody can reach, which is exactly what it was between A4 and A11b: shipped, titled, censused and rendered by nothing. SO THIS FRAME HAS TWO STATES AND NEITHER OF THEM TAKES A PASSWORD. Before: the address, and one action. After: no form at all, and a sentence that says where the mail goes, because the one thing somebody who just pressed the button needs is to know which window to look in next. THE AFTER-STATE SAYS "IF". Not politeness — the whole road is built so that an address with an account and an address without one get the same page and the same timing (`signin.request_reset`'s floor), and a sentence that said "we've sent you a link" would give away in words what the rest of it spends a measured 250 ms not giving away. THE BACK LINK CARRIES `next`. Somebody who arrived at the sign-in page on a deep link and detoured through here is still going where they were going. {% endcomment %}
